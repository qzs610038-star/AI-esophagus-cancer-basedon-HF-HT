# 双语图注

## 图 P2-3S｜固定空间臂下六种编码器的通路重建表现

**中文。** A、B 为患者—通路等权 PCC，分别显示内部验证和外部 XZY；C、D 为对应的整体展平 pooled PCC。六种编码器均使用固定空间臂，每种编码器有三次运行。空心标记和横向误差线表示均值 ± 样本标准差（SD），浅色点表示逐种子结果；点的纵向错开仅用于区分。患者—通路等权 PCC 先逐患者逐通路计算相关，再对患者和通路等权平均；整体展平 pooled PCC 将全部点位×通路的 z 分数展平后计算一次相关。外部 XZY 为一名患者。

**English.** **Figure P2-3S | Reconstruction performance of six encoders with the fixed spatial arm.** Panels A and B show patient–pathway equally weighted PCC for the internal validation and external XZY sets, respectively; panels C and D show the corresponding overall flattened pooled PCC. Each encoder has three runs with the spatial arm fixed. Open markers and horizontal error bars indicate the mean ± sample standard deviation (SD); pale points show individual runs, with vertical offsets used only for separation. Patient–pathway equally weighted PCC is computed per patient and pathway and then averaged with equal weight across patients and pathways. Overall flattened pooled PCC is calculated once after flattening all spot-by-pathway z scores. XZY represents one patient.

## 图 P2-5｜基线通路路线与任务二基因路线

**中文。** A、B 为患者—通路等权 PCC，分别显示内部验证和外部 XZY；C、D 为对应的整体展平 pooled PCC。UNI2-h 基线以既有 30 条通路目标（barcode-repair-v003）评估，含三次运行，图中显示均值 ± 样本标准差。任务二基因路线以 319 基因背景 ssGSEA 目标评估，含一次运行，图中显示该次运行值。患者—通路等权 PCC 先逐患者逐通路计算相关，再对患者和通路等权平均；整体展平 pooled PCC 将全部点位×通路的 z 分数展平后计算一次相关。外部 XZY 为一名患者。

**English.** **Figure P2-5 | Descriptive results for the pathway baseline and the task-2 gene route.** Panels A and B show patient–pathway equally weighted PCC for the internal validation and external XZY sets, respectively; panels C and D show the corresponding overall flattened pooled PCC. The UNI2-h baseline uses the existing 30-pathway target (barcode-repair-v003; three runs) and is shown as the mean ± sample standard deviation. The task-2 gene route uses the 319-gene-background ssGSEA target and is shown as a single-run value (one run). Patient–pathway equally weighted PCC is computed per patient and pathway and then averaged with equal weight across patients and pathways. Overall flattened pooled PCC is calculated once after flattening all spot-by-pathway z scores. XZY represents one patient.
